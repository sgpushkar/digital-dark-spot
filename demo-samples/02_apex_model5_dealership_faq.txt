Regional Dealership FAQ — Apex EV Model 5 Lineup
Publication Date: 2026-08-20

Q: What is the real-world battery range of the Apex Model 5?
A: Real-world highway range is tested at 460 km under moderate climate conditions, with city driving up to 500 km.

Q: How fast does the Apex Model 5 charge?
A: With standard 150 kW commercial DC fast chargers widely available across highway routes, the battery recharges from 10% to 80% in 28 minutes.

Q: What does the base model cost?
A: The introductory dealership ex-showroom price is ₹38,50,000 for domestic deliveries.

Q: Does the warranty cover the battery pack?
A: The high-voltage lithium battery pack is backed by a separate warranty of 8 years or 1,60,000 km. Specific capacity degradation thresholds must be verified with the regional service center upon delivery.
