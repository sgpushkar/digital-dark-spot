Hospital Clinical Advisory — Protocol RX-79
Issued: 14 October 2026
Target Cycle: 2026-27

1. Overview
Protocol RX-79 establishes clinical dosage and administration schedules for pediatric neuro-inflammation patients.

2. Dosage & Administration
The approved starting dosage is 15 mg per kilogram of body weight administered every 12 hours via intravenous infusion.

3. Monitoring Schedule
Blood serum concentrations and renal clearance rates must be monitored every 24 hours during the first 5 days of treatment.

4. Cost & Procurement
The hospital formulary price is ₹4,200 per vial, with patient assistance programs covering up to 50% for eligible low-income cases.

5. Warnings
Contraindicated for patients with acute hepatic impairment.
(Notice: Pediatric dosage adjustment for patients under 2 years of age is not documented in this advisory.)
