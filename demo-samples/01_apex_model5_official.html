Apex Model 5 Electric Sedan — Official Technical Overview

Published: 15 June 2026 | Last Revised: 01 September 2026

Range & Efficiency

The Apex Model 5 delivers an estimated driving range of 520 km on a single full charge under standard WLTP test cycles.

Fast Charging Capability

Equipped with 800V DC ultra-fast architecture, the Model 5 charges from 10% to 80% in 18 minutes using any certified 250 kW fast charger.

Pricing & Trims

Base trim pricing begins at ₹38,50,000 before federal clean energy incentives.

Vehicle Warranty

Apex Motors provides a bumper-to-bumper vehicle warranty of 5 years or 1,00,000 km, whichever occurs first.
